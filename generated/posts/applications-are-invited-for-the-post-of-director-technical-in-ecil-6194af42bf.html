I. COMPANY PROFILE Electronics Corporation of India Limited (ECIL) was incorporated in 1967 under the Indian Companies Act, 1956. ECIL is a Schedule- 'A',Miniratna Category-I CPSE with the administrative jurisdiction of Department of Atomic Energy. The main objectives of the company are to promote and develop Industrial electronics with indigenous know-how and to attain self-sufficiency in Atomic Energy programme, Defence, Aerospace, Nuclear, Security, IT & e-Governance and other sectors of strategic importance.
