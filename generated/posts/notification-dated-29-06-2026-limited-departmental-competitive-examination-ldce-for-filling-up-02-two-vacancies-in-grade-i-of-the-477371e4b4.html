NOTIFICATION Dated 29-06-2026: Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) for filling up 02(two) vacancies in Grade-I of the Tripura Judicial Service

📅 प्रकाशित: 29-06-2026   |   🏛 Tripura Judicial Service
The Thrombay Committee has announced the Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) to recruit two candidates for the Grade-I position in the Tripura Judicial Service. This examination is part of the regular recruitment process for judicial appointments in the state.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

The Thrombay Committee has issued a formal notification for the Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) aimed at filling two vacancies in the Grade-I category of the Tripura Judicial Service. This examination follows the standard procedure for selecting qualified individuals for judicial roles in the state.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • Two vacancies are available in the Grade-I category of the Tripura Judicial Service
  • The examination is conducted under the LDCE framework
  • Candidates must possess appropriate legal qualifications for judicial positions
  • The notification was officially dated 29-06-2026

🧭 आगे क्या करें?

Interested candidates should visit the official Thrombay Committee portal, register using Notice ID 26654, upload original self-attested documents including identity proof and educational certificates, and prepare for both written and interview components of the selection process.
