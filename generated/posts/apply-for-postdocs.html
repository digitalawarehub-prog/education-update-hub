Vacancy
Qualificationcriteria would not entitle one to be selected. The candidate is responsible for the correctness of the information provided in the application. If it is found at a later date that any information given in the application is incorrect or false, the candidature/appointment is liable to be cancelled/terminated. Candidates are encouraged to contact the chair of their preferred department/s for additional information. Selected candidates are expected to carry out their research work and other duties at IISc only. The IISc Post-Doctoral Fellowship is non-transferable. How to Apply: Candidates must first contact a faculty member in the Institute who is willing to host the candidate. A consent letter or consent email is required from the faculty member as a part of the application package. Candidates should apply online using the Post-Doctoral application portal. The following documents are required along with the application. (A) Curriculum Vitae with a list of all publications. (B) PDF files of at least two and up to five important publications. (C) Proposed research plan (up to 500 words). (D) Consent letter or email from faculty member. Please get in touch with the faculty member directly and obtain the consent of the faculty member for hosting you, before applying to the portal. (E) PDF file of PhD degree certificate, or provisional PhD degree awarded certificate, or PhD thesis submitted certificate. (F) Any other relevant information the applicant may want to furnish. (G) Candidates can apply throughout the year. However, applications will be reviewed quarterly, i.e., 31 March, 30 June, 30 September, and 31 December. Candidates can expect a decision on their application within six weeks of the aforementioned review dates. For any further information related to this, please contact: The Office of Deans of Divisions, Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore – 560 012. Email: office.divdeans@iisc.ac.in Phone: +91-80-22932478; +91-80-22932286 Selection Process: Candidates must apply on or before the review dates mentioned above. The applications will be screened, and a small subset of applicants will be called for an interview either in person or over video conference before a final decision is made.
