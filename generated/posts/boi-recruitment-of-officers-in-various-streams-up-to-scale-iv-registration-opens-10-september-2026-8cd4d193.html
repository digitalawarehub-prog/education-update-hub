BOI Recruitment of Officers in Various Streams up to Scale IV – Registration Opens 10 September 2026

📅 प्रकाशित: 16-09-2026   |   🏛 Bank of India
Bank of India has released a recruitment notification for officers in various streams up to Scale IV under Project No. 2026-27/02. The online registration window opens on 10 September 2026 and closes on 25 September 2026, with fee payment allowed during the same period.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

Bank of India (BOI) has announced a fresh recruitment drive for officers across multiple streams up to Scale IV. The notification, referenced as Project No. 2026-27/02, outlines the key dates for online application and fee payment. Candidates interested in banking careers at the officer level should note the registration timeline and prepare their documents accordingly. The application process is entirely online, and the bank has provided a dedicated portal for submission. This article breaks down the critical dates and steps to help applicants navigate the process smoothly.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • Recruitment for officers in various streams up to Scale IV (Project No. 2026-27/02).
  • Online registration commences on 10 September 2026.
  • Registration closes on 25 September 2026; editing of application details also closes on the same day.
  • Last date for printing the application is 10 October 2026.
  • Online fee payment window: 10 September 2026 to 25 September 2026.
  • Applications are subject to scrutiny and can be rejected if found ineligible at any stage.
  • The portal is best viewed on Internet Explorer 9+, Google Chrome 30+, Firefox 20+.

🧭 आगे क्या करें?

To apply, visit the official IBPS registration portal for BOI (https://ibpsreg.ibps.in/boiaug26) during the registration window. Click on the 'New Registration' link, provide basic details to generate a provisional registration number and password. Log in with the credentials, fill in the application form with personal, educational, and professional details as required. Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, and other documents as per specifications. Pay the application fee online through the available payment modes. Review the entered data carefully before final submission, as editing is only allowed until 25 September 2026. After submission, download and print the application form for future reference.

❓ अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

When does the online registration for BOI officer recruitment start?

Online registration begins on 10 September 2026.

What is the last date to submit the application?

The registration closes on 25 September 2026; no applications will be accepted after this date.

Can I edit my application after submission?

Yes, editing of application details is allowed until 25 September 2026, the same as the registration closure date.

What is the last date to print the submitted application?

Candidates can print their application until 10 October 2026.

Is the application fee paid separately?

The fee payment window is the same as the registration period, from 10 September to 25 September 2026, and must be completed online.
