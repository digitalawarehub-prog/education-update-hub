Go Back Register as New User What's New Announcements Recruitment Agencies The Government of India has decided for making available publically scores of non-recommended willing candidates in the final examination (interview) of the Government recruiting agencies (UPSC, RRB, DRDO,etc.) through NCS portal. This decision is with the view to provide a useful database to other employers including private to enable them to identify good employable candidates.
