Hall‑Ticket Download for A.P. High Court Recruitment (Notification No.01/2026)

📅 प्रकाशित: 05-09-2026   |   🏛 High Court of Andhra Pradesh
The High Court of Andhra Pradesh has issued several recruitment notifications for 2026, including 16 Civil Judges (Senior Division) posts, 41 Civil Judge (Junior Division) appointments, 4 Law Clerk posts on contract, and various District Judiciary staff positions, with hall‑ticket download links available.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

हIGH COURT OF ANDHRA PRADESH ने 2026 में विभिन्न पदों के लिए recruitment नोटिफिकेशन जारी किए हैं, जिसमें सिविल जज, लॉ क्लर्क और जिला न्यायालय के अन्य कर्मचारी शामिल हैं, और हॉल‑टिकट डाउनलोड के लिए लिंक उपलब्ध कराए गए हैं।

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • 16 Civil Judges (Senior Division) posts are being recruited through a Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE)
  • 41 candidates have been appointed as Civil Judge (Junior Division) as per G.O. dated 27.08.2026
  • 4 Law Clerk posts on contract basis have been notified, with Viva Voce scheduled from 17/08/2026 to 21/08/2026
  • Hall‑Ticket download links are provided for the recruitment under Notification No.01/2026 dated 20.03.2026

सीमित विभागीय प्रतिस्पर्धात्मक परीक्षा (LDCE) के माध्यम से चयन किया जाएगा।
