NOTIFICATION Dated 07-09-2026: Reschedule of written examination for recruitment to Grade-I of the TJS in pursuance to Advt. No.TJS/Grade-I/01/2026, dated 20.06.2026 and final lists of eligible and ineligible candidates.

📅 प्रकाशित: 07-09-2026   |   🏛 Tripura High Court
The Tripura High Court (THC) has issued a notification dated 07-09-2026 regarding the rescheduling of the written examination for recruitment to Grade-I of the Tripura Judicial Service (TJS) under Advertisement No. TJS/Grade-I/01/2026 dated 20.06.2026. The notice also includes the final lists of eligible and ineligible candidates who appeared for the said examination. Candidates are advised to check the official website for updated exam dates and their inclusion status in the merit or eligible lists.

❓ अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

What is the purpose of this notification?

This notification informs candidates about the rescheduling of the written examination for recruitment to Grade-I of the Tripura Judicial Service and publishes the final lists of eligible and ineligible candidates.

Where can I find the revised exam date?

The revised exam date will be mentioned in the official notification available on the Tripura High Court website at https://thc.tripura.gov.in.

How can I check if I am eligible or ineligible?

The final lists of eligible and ineligible candidates are published in the notification. Candidates should search for their names in the respective lists.

Do I need to reapply due to the rescheduling?

No, candidates who have already applied do not need to reapply. The rescheduling applies to the already submitted applications.

What should ineligible candidates do?

Ineligible candidates should review the reasons stated in the list and contact the concerned authority if they wish to appeal or seek clarification.
