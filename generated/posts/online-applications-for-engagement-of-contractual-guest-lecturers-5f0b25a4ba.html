Online applications for engagement of Contractual/Guest Lecturers

📅 प्रकाशित: 12-09-2026   |   🏛 Faculty of Agriculture, Wadura; Faculty of Horticulture, Shalimar; Faculty of Veterinary Science & AH, Shuhama; College of Agricultural Engineering & Technology, CAIML AI & Machine Learning; Faculty of Fisheries; Faculty of Temperate Sericulture, Mirgund
SKUAST-Kashmir has opened recruitment for Guest Lecturer positions for Academic Year 2026-27 across the listed faculties and constituent colleges. The official portal uses Google Sign-In, a master profile system and ICICI EazyPay for fee payment. The application portal is currently closed.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

The official SKUAST-Kashmir recruitment portal is meant for online applications to Contractual/Guest Lecturer positions in Academic Year 2026-27. Candidates can sign in with Google, prepare a master profile, choose disciplines, pay the fee and print the final form, but the portal is currently closed.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • Recruitment is for Guest Lecturer positions for Academic Year 2026-27.
  • Google Sign-In provides secure entry without a separate password.
  • The master profile allows candidates to apply for multiple posts.
  • The application fee is ₹500 for each discipline applied.
  • Payments are handled securely through ICICI EazyPay.
  • Candidates must upload a photo and signature and enter academic and experience details.

❓ अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

How can I apply for the SKUAST-Kashmir Guest Lecturer posts?

Sign in with Google, build your profile, select the desired disciplines, pay the fee and print the final form. The portal is currently closed.

What is the application fee?

The application fee is ₹500 for each discipline applied.

Can I use one profile for multiple posts?

Yes. The master profile system allows you to fill in your details once and apply to multiple posts.
