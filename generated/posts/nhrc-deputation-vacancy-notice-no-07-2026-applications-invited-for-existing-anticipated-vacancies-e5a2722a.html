NHRC Deputation Vacancy Notice No. 07/2026 – Applications Invited for Existing/Anticipated Vacancies

📅 प्रकाशित: 14-09-2026   |   🏛 National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)
The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) has issued Deputation Vacancy Notice No. 07/2026 inviting applications from eligible candidates to fill up existing and anticipated vacancies on a deputation basis. The notice was last updated on 14-09-2026 and applications are being accepted from 23-07-2026 onwards.
