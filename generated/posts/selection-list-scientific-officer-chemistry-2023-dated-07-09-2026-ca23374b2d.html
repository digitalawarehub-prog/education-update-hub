❓ अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

How can I check my name in the selection list?

Visit the official MPPSC website at https://mppsc.mp.gov.in and download the selection list PDF. You can search for your name using your registration or roll number.

Is the selection list final?

No, the selection list is provisional. Final selection will be confirmed after document verification as per the schedule announced by MPPSC.

What documents are required for document verification?

Candidates should carry original documents along with self-attested copies of educational certificates, category certificate, and other relevant documents as specified in the official notification.

What should I do if there is an error in my name or category?

If you find any discrepancy in your name or category details, contact the MPPSC office immediately through the official contact details provided on their website.
