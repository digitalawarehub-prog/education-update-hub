NOTIFICATION Dated 11-08-2026: Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) for filling up the vacancies in Grade-I of Tripura Judicial Service will be held on 26.09.2026 (4th Saturday) and 27.09.2026 (Sunday)

📅 प्रकाशित: 11-08-2026   |   🏛 Tripura Judicial Service
The Tripura High Court has announced a Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) to fill vacancies in Grade-I of the Tripura Judicial Service. The examination is scheduled for 26th and 27th September 2026. The notification was issued on 11th August 2026. Eligible departmental candidates can apply through the official website.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

The Tripura High Court has released a notification for the Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) to fill Grade-I vacancies in the Tripura Judicial Service. The exam will be conducted on 26th and 27th September 2026. This is a departmental examination open to eligible serving officers.
