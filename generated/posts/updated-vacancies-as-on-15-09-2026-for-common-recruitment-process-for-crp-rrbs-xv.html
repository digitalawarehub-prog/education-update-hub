Updated Vacancies as on 15.09.2026 for Common Recruitment Process for CRP-RRBs-XV

📅 प्रकाशित: 15-09-2026   |   🏛 Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)
The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has published an updated vacancy list for the Common Recruitment Process for Regional Rural Banks (CRP-RRBs-XV) effective 15 September 2026. The annexure provides the latest vacancy positions across participating banks.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

IBPS has released an updated vacancy annexure for the CRP-RRBs-XV recruitment cycle. The document, dated 15 September 2026, revises the number of openings for various posts in Regional Rural Banks. Aspirants should download the PDF from the official IBPS website to view the bank-wise and category-wise vacancy details. This update is crucial for candidates who have already applied or are planning to apply, as vacancy numbers directly affect competition and cut-offs.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • Updated vacancy list for CRP-RRBs-XV released on 15 September 2026.
  • Annexure available on IBPS official website (PDF).
  • Covers vacancies for Officer Scale I, II, III and Office Assistant posts.
  • Vacancy figures are revised and may differ from earlier notifications.
  • Candidates must refer to the PDF for bank-wise and category-wise breakdown.
  • No changes to eligibility, age limit, or application process mentioned in this update.
  • Further recruitment stages will follow the original CRP-RRBs-XV schedule.

🧭 आगे क्या करें?

Visit the IBPS website (www.ibps.in) and navigate to the 'CRP-RRBs-XV' section. Click on the link for 'Annexure-I Updated Vacancies as on 15.09.2026' to download the PDF. Open the file and locate the table for your desired post and bank. Note the vacancy numbers for your category (UR, OBC, SC, ST, EWS). Use this information to assess competition and prepare accordingly.

❓ अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

Where can I find the updated vacancy PDF?

The PDF is hosted on the IBPS website under the CRP-RRBs-XV recruitment section, titled 'Annexure-I Updated Vacancies as on 15.09.2026'.

Does this update change the application deadline?

No, the vacancy update does not modify the application schedule. Refer to the original notification for the last date to apply.

Are the vacancies final?

Vacancies are provisional and subject to change as per bank requirements. The final vacancy may differ at the time of allotment.

Do I need to re-apply after this update?

No, candidates who have already applied need not re-apply. The update is for information only.

Which posts are covered in this annexure?

The annexure includes vacancies for Officer Scale I, Officer Scale II (Generalist and Specialist), Officer Scale III, and Office Assistant (Multipurpose).
