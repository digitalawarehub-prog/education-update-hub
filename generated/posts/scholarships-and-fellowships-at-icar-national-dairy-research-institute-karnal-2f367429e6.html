Scholarships and Fellowships at ICAR-National Dairy Research Institute, Karnal

📅 प्रकाशित: 11-09-2026   |   🏛 Scholarship Section, NDRI
Details of merit-based scholarships/fellowships for B.Tech, Master's, Ph.D. and in-service candidates at NDRI, including amounts, eligibility, and conditions.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

NDRI offers various scholarships and fellowships to support students in dairy and allied sciences programmes.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • National Talent Scholarship: Rs.3000/month for UG merit students outside domicile state
  • Institute Scholarship: Master Rs.7560/month + Rs.6000/year contingency; Ph.D. Rs.25000-28000/month + Rs.10000/year contingency
  • ICAR JRF/SRF: Master Rs.12640/month; Ph.D. Rs.37000-42000/month + Rs.10000/year contingency
  • In-service Ph.D. scholarship: Rs.3000/month + contingency under ICAR/SAU conditions
  • Continued payment requires ≥6.5 GPA or 65% marks; SC/ST relaxation up to 5% once
  • Scholarship terminated if student leaves rolls, completes study, or receives other financial aid

❓ अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

Who is eligible for the National Talent Scholarship?

UG merit students joining NDRI from outside their domicile state, not receiving any other scholarship, maintaining OGPA ≥7.0.

What is the monthly amount for Master's Institute Scholarship?

Rs.7560 per month plus Rs.6000 per annum contingency.

Can in-service Ph.D. candidates receive the Institute Scholarship?

Yes, Rs.3000 per month plus contingency if employed under ICAR/SAU and joining from a different institute.

Is there an age limit for these scholarships?

The source does not specify any age limit.

How is scholarship continuation decided?

Based on ≥6.5 GPA or 65% marks in the previous semester; SC/ST candidates may get a one-time 5% relaxation.
