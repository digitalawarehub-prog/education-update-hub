Recruitment of university Professor-cum-chief Scientist (Regular),Advt.No.-10/2023

📅 प्रकाशित: 22-03-2023   |   🏛 Jharkhand Public Service Commission
The Jharkhand Public Service Commission invites applications for the University Professor-cum-Chief Scientist (Regular) position, advertised as No. 10/2023. Candidates meeting the eligibility criteria are encouraged to submit their applications before the stipulated deadline.
