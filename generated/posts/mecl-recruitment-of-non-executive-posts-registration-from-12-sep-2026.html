MECL Recruitment of Non-Executive Posts Registration From 12-Sep-2026

📅 प्रकाशित: 16-09-2026   |   🏛 IBPS
MECL has opened online registration for non‑executive posts starting 12‑09‑2026 and closing on 11‑10‑2026. Applicants must submit the form, pay the fee within the same period, and can edit their details up to the closure date.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

Mineral Exploration Corporation Limited (MECL) has announced the schedule for its non‑executive recruitment drive. The online application window runs from 12 September 2026 to 11 October 2026, during which candidates can register, pay the required fee, and make corrections to their submitted data. The portal also allows applicants to print their completed forms until 26 October 2026. The notification stresses that submission does not guarantee eligibility; all applications will be scrutinised and may be rejected if any criterion is not met. The system is best viewed on Internet Explorer 9+, Google Chrome 30+, or Firefox 20+.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • Online registration opens on 12‑09‑2026 and closes on 11‑10‑2026.
  • Fee payment is accepted only between 12‑09‑2026 and 11‑10‑2026.
  • Applicants can edit application details up to 11‑10‑2026.
  • Printed copy of the application can be generated until 26‑10‑2026.
  • The portal supports IE 9+, Chrome 30+, Firefox 20+.
  • Submission is provisional; applications are subject to later scrutiny and rejection if ineligible.
