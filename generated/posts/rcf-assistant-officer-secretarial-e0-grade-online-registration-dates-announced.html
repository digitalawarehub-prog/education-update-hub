RCF Assistant Officer (Secretarial) E0 Grade Online Registration Dates Announced

📅 प्रकाशित: Not specified in the supplied source.   |   🏛 RCF
Candidates can register online for the Assistant Officer (Secretarial) E0 Grade post from 10-09-2026 at 08:00 AM until 26-09-2026 at 05:00 PM. The same window applies to online fee payment, while application editing closes on 26-09-2026 and printing remains available until 11-10-2026.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

The update establishes the online application timetable for RCF's Assistant Officer (Secretarial) E0 Grade recruitment. Applicants should complete registration and payment within the stated deadlines, then review the notification and related guidance because submitting an online form does not establish that all eligibility requirements have been met.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • Online registration commences on 10-09-2026 at 08:00 AM.
  • Registration closes on 26-09-2026 at 05:00 PM.
  • Online fee payment is available from 10-09-2026 at 08:00 AM through 26-09-2026 at 05:00 PM.
  • Editing application details closes on 26-09-2026 at 05:00 PM.
  • Applicants can print their applications until 11-10-2026 at 05:00 PM.
  • Applications remain subject to later scrutiny and may be rejected if an applicant is found ineligible at any stage.
