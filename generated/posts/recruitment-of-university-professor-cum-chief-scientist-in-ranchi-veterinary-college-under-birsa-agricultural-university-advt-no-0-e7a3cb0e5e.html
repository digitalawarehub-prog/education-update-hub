Recruitment of University Professor-Cum-Chief Scientist in Ranchi Veterinary College Under Birsa Agricultural University, Advt. No. 08/2017

📅 प्रकाशित: 20-04-2017   |   🏛 Jharkhand Public Service Commission
The Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) has announced a recruitment for the position of Professor-Cum-Chief Scientist at Ranchi Veterinary College under Birsa Agricultural University. Applications were invited through the Online Application Form (OAS) with a deadline for document verification on 22-03-2018. The selection process includes document verification and interview, with a provisional list of candidates released on 08-01-2020.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

The Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) has issued Advertisement No. 08/2017 for the recruitment of Professor-Cum-Chief Scientist at Ranchi Veterinary College, which is affiliated with Birsa Agricultural University. Candidates were required to apply online through the OAS portal, with document verification scheduled for March 2018. The selection process involves verifying documents and conducting an interview.
