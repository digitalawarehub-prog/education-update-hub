Reservation for various categories shall be applicable in accordance with the provisions of the Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Rules, 2005 read with S.O. 176 of 2024 dated 15.03.2024 and S.O. 305 of 2024 dated 21.05.2024. A candidate seeking consideration under any reserved category must be in possession of a valid category certificate as on the cutoff date. Category certificates issued after the cutoff date shall in no case be entertained. In-service University employees must fill the online form and also send their Application Form through proper channel to Registrar, SKUAST-K, Shalimar, Srinagar-190025 by or before 2 August 2026 (5:00 PM) . All advertised positions are available at various AICRP Centres; appointment, pay, transfers and career advancement shall be governed by the relevant ICAR project rules. Continuation of posts depends on continuation of ICAR funding. Original certificates and testimonials together with the printout copy of the application form submitted online shall be required to be produced at the time of verification of documents. The number of post(s) indicated is tentative and subject to increase or decrease. The University reserves the right to withdraw or defer filling up of any or all post(s). Candidates wishing to apply for more than one post must submit separate Application Forms along with the prescribed fees for each post. Candidates shall necessarily indicate their personal mobile No. and active e-mail address in the relevant column of the Application Form for any communication/clarification. No TA/DA will be paid to the candidates for participation in the test or interview. In case of any dispute, the jurisdiction for legal proceedings shall be restricted to the Courts at Srinagar, UT of J&K only.
