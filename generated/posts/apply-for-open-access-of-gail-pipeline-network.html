What is the difference between Common Carrier Capacity and Contract Carrier Capacity? A common carrier system implies that capacity in a natural gas pipeline, over and above the pipeline entity’s own requirement and capacity allocated on a contract carrier basis, available to any other entity on a non-discriminatory “first-come-first served” basis subject to the latter entering into a contract for transporting a volume of natural gas normally for a period of less than one year. A contract carrier system implies the capacity in a natural gas pipeline, over and above the pipeline entity’s own requirements, available to any other entity subject to execution of a firm contract for transportation of a volume of natural gas for a period of minimum one year, on mutually agreed terms and conditions. 2. How do I login into the website for booking capacity under Common Carrier Capacity? To login into the portal for raising CT booking request, Password shall be provided to existing shippers through E-Mail. Existing customers shall use customer ID (SAP code) and this Password to login into the website for raising request for booking common carrier capacity. 3. How CT shall be provided from GAIL? After CT requisition has been raised in the website, a unique system generated CT requisition number shall be provided. The CT requisition shall subsequently flow to concerned Zonal Office of GAIL, who shall process the CT requisition further. 4. How to book Contract Carrier Capacity in GAIL’s pipeline? For booking contract carrier capacity, please contact Corporate Office of GAIL. 5. Is payment securitization required? Yes, payment securitization is required. One of the following payment securitization has to be provided by customer: Letter of Credit (LC) to be provided for value of 2 fortnights of gas transmission. Value of one fortnight is 16 days transmission tariff of gas at MDQ plus any other charges and taxes & duties there on. Aggregate liability of issuing bank shall be two times the face value of LC Format of LC shall be as per Format C of GTA. Bank Guarantee (BG) to be provided for value of 3 fortnights of gas transmission. Value of one fortnight is 16 days transmission tariff of gas at MDQ plus any other charges and taxes & duties there on. Mix of BG for one fortnight and SD for two fortnight value. How to request for booking of capacity, if my plant is not connected to GAIL’s pipeline? Please fill up the form for new customer and GAIL shall contact you at the earliest. 7. Can my CT request get rejected after raising CT request? Yes, CT request can get rejected on the following grounds Requested capacity is not available in the pipeline Adequate payment securitization as per GTA is not provided Details filled in CT requisition is incomplete or inadequate Customer has not signed and sent the CT received through e-mail. 8. Can a CT requisition modified after submission? Yes, a CT requisition can be modified until Zonal office has not processed it. 9. How New Customer shall be onboarded to book Common Carrier Capacity in GAIL pipeline? A new customer shall fill up a form by clicking New Customer tab and shall provide details of the company , contact person, plant location, capacity requirement details etc. After submission of the form, GAIL shall contact within 3 working days and shall guide further for signing of requisite agreements.
