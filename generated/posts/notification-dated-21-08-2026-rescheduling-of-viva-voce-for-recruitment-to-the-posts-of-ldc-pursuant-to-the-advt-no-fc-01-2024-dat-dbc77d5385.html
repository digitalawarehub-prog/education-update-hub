NOTIFICATION Dated 21-08-2026: Rescheduling of Viva Voce for recruitment to the posts of LDC pursuant to the Advt. No. FC 01/2024, dated 29.11.2024

📅 प्रकाशित: 21-08-2026   |   🏛 Recruitment Department
This notification rescinds the previously scheduled Viva Voce examination for LDC (Local District Civil Services) recruitment posts and sets a new date for the test.

📝 संक्षिप्त विवरण

The State Civil Services Commission (SCSC) of Tripura has issued a formal notification regarding the rescheduling of the Viva Voce examination for recruitment to Local District Civil Services (LDC) posts. This update modifies the original timeline established under Advance Notice FC 01/2024.

📌 मुख्य बिंदु

  • The Viva Voce examination for LDC recruitment has been officially rescheduled
  • Original schedule was 29.11.2024 per Advance Notice FC 01/2024
  • New commencement date is 21-08-2026 at 2:30 PM
  • Reference Number: Notice ID 26725
  • Official source: thc.tripura.gov.in
