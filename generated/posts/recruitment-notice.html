Home Careers Rewarding Careers that help build the Nation Working towards a common mission Incepted with the mission to serve the farmers in ensuring their prosperity; the IFFCO family has worked tirelessly over the past five decades to produce energy efficient fertilizers, imparting technical knowledge, establishing sustainable practices and assisting the farmers in achieving self-reliance. Our mission to ensure the farmer’s prosperity is what drives each one of us here at IFFCO. It is the undying passion of our people and their resolve to contribute towards the nation that has shaped many illustrious careers at IFFCO.
