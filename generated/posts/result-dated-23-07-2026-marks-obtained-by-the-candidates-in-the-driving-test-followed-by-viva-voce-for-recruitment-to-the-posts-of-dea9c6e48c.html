RESULT Dated 23-07-2026: Marks obtained by the candidates in the Driving Test followed by Viva Voce for recruitment to the posts of Driver in the establishments of the District & Subordinate Judiciary of Tripura in pursuance of the Advertisement No. DC 01/2025, dated 23.05.2025.

📅 प्रकाशित: 23-05-2025   |   🏛 Tripura High Court
The Tripura High Court has released the results for the Driving Test and Viva Voce examination conducted under Advertisement No. DC 01/2025 for Driver recruitment in District & Subordinate Judiciary establishments. Candidates can check their performance and marks on the official website. The selection process included a practical driving test followed by a viva voce interview.
