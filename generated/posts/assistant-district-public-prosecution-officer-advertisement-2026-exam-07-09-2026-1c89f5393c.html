📅 प्रकाशित: 07-09-2026   |   🏛 Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission
The Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission has issued a corrigendum notice for the Assistant District Public Prosecution Officer recruitment examination 2026. The notification, dated 07 September 2026, states that the exam will be conducted on 01 November 2026. Candidates can apply online from 18 October 2026 until 21 October 2026.
